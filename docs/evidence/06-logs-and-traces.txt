## 2. Key Vault audit log: who asked for the secret, from where, and what the vault said

Read it top to bottom. Terraform writes the secret through the control plane. The app's own
identity is then REFUSED three times when the platform reaches the vault from the App Service's
public address, and ALLOWED when the same identity arrives from 10.60.1.x, the app subnet, over
the private endpoint. Same identity, same secret: the only difference is the network path.

The last four rows are the break-in attempt in 05-data-tier-private.txt: refused first by RBAC,
and then, once the role had been granted, by the network.
